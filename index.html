Anatomy of a Terra

Four parts. One easy stride.

01

Breathable mesh upper

An open knit that lets heat out and air in, so your feet stay fresh from morning to night.

02

Contoured insole

A gently shaped footbed that supports your arch and settles into your step over time.

03

Cushioned midsole

Three layers of soft foam that absorb each landing and give some of it back when you push off.

04

Grip outsole

Deep lugs and durable rubber for confident traction on pavement, tiles and wet ground.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do they fit?

Most people find Terra true to size. If you are between sizes, go up half a size for a roomier fit.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 1 to 2 working days. You will get a tracking update as soon as your pair is on its way.

Can I return them?

Yes. Unworn pairs in their original box can be returned within 30 days.

How do I clean them?

Wipe with a damp cloth and a little mild soap, then let them air-dry away from direct heat.
